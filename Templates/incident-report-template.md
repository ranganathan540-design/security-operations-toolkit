## Reporting Principles

A professional incident report should be:

- **Factual** — record what was observed or reliably reported.
- **Clear** — use simple and precise language.
- **Chronological** — describe events in the order they occurred.
- **Objective** — distinguish facts from opinions.
- **Timely** — complete the report as soon as reasonably possible.
- **Confidential** — share information only with authorized persons.

Never deliberately alter, fabricate, or omit material facts in an incident report.
