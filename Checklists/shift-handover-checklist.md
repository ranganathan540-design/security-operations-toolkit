- [ ] No known emergency equipment issue is pending

## Important Note

This checklist is a general open-source template.

Organizations should modify it according to their:

- Site-specific security requirements
- Risk assessment
- Client instructions
- Emergency procedures
- Applicable laws and regulations
- Internal policies

Do not include confidential access credentials, passwords, personal data, or sensitive security information in publicly shared copies of this checklist.
